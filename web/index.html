Your agent shops. You approve. PayPal pays.

Agentic PayPal lets Claude or Codex buy goods for you from any PayPal merchant. Small purchases are paid at once from a balance you fund in advance, up to a limit you set. Larger ones wait for your approval in PayPal.

How it works

  1. Log in with PayPal

    Logging in links your PayPal account to your agent. Only that account can approve its purchases.

  2. Ask your agent to buy

    The agent places the order with the merchant, for anything a PayPal merchant sells.

  3. Approve only the big ones

    Purchases within your limit are paid at once from your balance. Larger ones wait for your approval in PayPal.

Add it to your agent

Add this to your Claude Desktop configuration, with your own keys, then restart Claude.

{
  "mcpServers": {
    "agentic-paypal": {
      "command": "node",
      "args": ["/absolute/path/to/agentic-paypal/dist/index.js"],
      "env": { "THIRDWEB_SECRET_KEY": "", "PAYPAL_CLIENT_ID": "", "PAYPAL_CLIENT_SECRET": "" }
    }
  }
}
